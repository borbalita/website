    About me

    I'm Borbála Tasnádi, founder of Tasnadi AI and a freelance AI Engineer based in Berlin, Germany. I help companies and teams get past AI hype and into systems that run reliably in production. I design and build GenAI systems tailored to your existing infrastructure — RAG-based knowledge bases, agentic workflows, document parsing and extraction tools, and automation pipelines that replace hours of manual work with something that runs on its own. My approach is grounded in engineering fundamentals, not just prompting. Solid architecture, testing, and monitoring, built to last.

    My tech stack includes Python, LangChain, LangGraph, PydanticAI, FastAPI, Django, vector databases (Pinecone, Chroma, pgvector), Postgres, Docker, AWS, Azure, and GCP, with Langfuse and Phoenix (Arize) for LLM observability and evaluation.

    Want to get started? Here's how I typically work:

    🔍 Discovery: Understanding your data, workflows, and where AI can create real leverage.

    ⚙️ Solution design: Architecting a system built around your existing infrastructure and knowledge base.

    🔄 Implementation: Building and integrating the solution with careful attention to reliability and edge cases.

    📈 Optimization: Monitoring and refining performance once it's live.
